## 1. Overview

Platform-X generates revenue from tenants (hotels, restaurants, entertainment, agencies) through a combination of:

1. **Subscription fees** — monthly/yearly platform access
2. **Transaction commission** — % of each booking
3. **Premium features** — add-ons beyond base plan
4. **Setup/onboarding fees** — one-time onboarding cost

No revenue from end-users directly. The platform is free for guests.

## 3. Revenue Sources

### 3.1 Subscription Revenue

Recurring monthly/yearly fee per tenant.

**Pros:**
- Predictable
- Scalable
- Not dependent on transaction volume

**Cons:**
- Tenants may prefer commission-only
- Requires active sales

### 3.2 Commission Revenue

Percentage of each booking made through the platform.

**Pros:**
- Aligned with tenant success
- Tenants happy when they get bookings
- Scales with tenant growth

**Cons:**
- Requires tracking
- Legal complexity for split payments
- Income fluctuates

### 3.3 Premium Features

Add-ons for higher tiers:
- Custom domain
- Advanced analytics
- AI concierge customization
- Multi-language expansion
- Custom integrations

### 3.4 Onboarding Fees

One-time fee for setup:
- Data migration
- Branding setup
- Staff training
- Custom configuration

---

## 4. Guest-Side (Free)

End-users (guests, travelers) use the platform for FREE.

The platform does NOT charge guests for:
- Browsing
- Booking
- Account creation
- Loyalty program

**Why free for guests?**
- Direct competition with Booking.com (which is free for users)
- Volume drives tenant value
- Data insights come from scale

---

## 5. Competitive Analysis

### Booking.com / Agoda / Expedia

**Their model:**
- Free for hotels to list
- Commission 15-25% per booking
- Full control of customer data
- Hotels become dependent

**Our model:**
- Subscription + lower commission (5-10%)
- Hotel owns customer data
- Platform is a tool, not a gatekeeper
- Multi-service (hotels + dining + entertainment + flights)

**Our advantages:**
- Lower cost for tenants
- Better tech (AI, multi-surface)
- Regional focus (UAE/GCC first)
- Multi-category (not just hotels)

### Other SaaS Platforms

**Examples:** SiteMinder, Cloudbeds, Mews

**Difference:**
- They target hotel operations (PMS)
- We target guest-facing experience + bookings
- We're multi-tenant + multi-category
- We're regional-first (Arabic, Gulf market)

## 10. Pricing Strategy Principles

1. **Value-based** — price based on what tenants save/gain
2. **Transparent** — no hidden fees
3. **Flexible** — subscription + commission mix
4. **Scalable** — pricing grows with tenant success
5. **Regional** — competitive with local providers
