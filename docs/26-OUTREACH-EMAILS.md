## Personalization guidance

For each recipient, customize:

1. **Opening line** — reference something specific (a recent
   announcement, an expansion, a public statement from their CTO).
2. **Why them** — one sentence on why this company, not another.
3. **Sign-off** — use a name you know if possible; otherwise,
   "Dear [Title]".

## Timing

- **Send:** Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11 AM local time.
- **Avoid:** Friday afternoons, weekends, Ramadan iftar hours.
- **Follow-up:** 10 days after the first email, once.
- **Move on:** if no response after follow-up, deprioritize and
  re-engage after 60 days.

## Tracking

Maintain a simple tracking sheet:

| Company | Contact | Date sent | Opened? | Replied? | NDA? | IM sent? | Status |
|---------|---------|-----------|---------|----------|------|----------|--------|

## Red flags in replies

If a reply asks for:

- **Free technical consulting before an NDA** — decline politely.
- **Full repository access without an NDA** — decline.
- **Payment in installments without escrow** — decline.
- **Detailed customer data** — there is none; the platform has no
  live tenants yet.
