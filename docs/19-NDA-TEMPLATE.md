> granting repository access to a prospective buyer.**

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential acquisition of the software
platform known as **Platform-X** (the "Transaction"). In connection
with the Transaction, each Party may disclose to the other certain
confidential and proprietary information. This Agreement governs the
handling of that information.

### 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one
Party (the "Discloser") to the other Party (the "Recipient"), whether
oral, written, electronic, or in any other form, that is designated
as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the
circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

- Source code, architecture, technical designs, and documentation.
- Business plans, financial projections, pricing, and customer data.
- Trade secrets, algorithms, and proprietary methodologies.
- The existence and terms of any ongoing or potential Transaction.
- Any information about the Seller's AI-driven development
  methodology.

### 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the
    Recipient;

(b) was in the Recipient's possession before disclosure, as
    evidenced by written records;

(c) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or
    reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information; or

(d) is rightfully obtained by the Recipient from a third party
    without breach of any obligation of confidentiality.

### 4. Obligations of the Recipient

The Recipient agrees to:

(a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence;

(b) use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of
    evaluating the Transaction;

(c) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party
    without the Discloser's prior written consent, except to the
    Recipient's professional advisors (legal, financial, technical)
    who need to know and who are bound by confidentiality
    obligations at least as protective as this Agreement;

(d) protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree
    of care it uses to protect its own confidential information,
    and in no event less than reasonable care;

(e) not reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software or
    materials provided;

(f) promptly notify the Discloser of any unauthorized use or
    disclosure of Confidential Information.

### 5. Term

This Agreement remains in effect for **three (3) years** from the
Effective Date. The obligations of confidentiality survive the
termination of this Agreement for the full term.

### 6. Return or Destruction of Materials

Upon the Discloser's written request, or upon termination of
discussions, the Recipient shall promptly return or destroy all
Confidential Information in its possession and certify such return
or destruction in writing. The Recipient may retain one archival
copy solely for legal compliance purposes.

### 7. No License or Transfer of Rights

Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any license,
ownership, or other right in or to the Discloser's Confidential
Information, intellectual property, or the Platform-X platform.

### 8. No Obligation to Proceed

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to enter into the
Transaction or any other agreement.

### 9. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that a breach of this Agreement may cause
irreparable harm for which monetary damages are inadequate. The
Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any
other remedies available at law or equity.

### 10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **Dubai International
Financial Centre (DIFC)**, United Arab Emirates. Any dispute arising
out of or in connection with this Agreement is subject to the
exclusive jurisdiction of the **DIFC Courts**.

### 11. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties
regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior
understandings, whether written or oral, relating to that subject
matter.

### 12. Amendments

Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by
both Parties.

### 13. Counterparts and Electronic Signatures

This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by
electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all
of which together constitute one and the same instrument.

## Notes for the Seller (do not send to buyer)

- Keep a signed PDF of every NDA before granting repository access.
- If the buyer is a large corporate, expect them to use their own
  NDA template — this is normal. Review their template with a lawyer
  before signing.
- This template is deliberately conservative. If the buyer pushes
  back, the negotiable points are: (a) term length (3 years),
  (b) governing law (DIFC), (c) return/destruction timelines.
- For very large transactions (above USD 1M), consider engaging a
  UAE or DIFC-licensed M&A lawyer for the full sale agreement.
