# Platform-X — Target Buyers List

> **Purpose:** A prioritized list of 25 strategic buyers in the MENA
> hospitality and technology sector who could acquire Platform-X.
>
> **Contact fields are placeholders.** The seller (or their advisor)
> must complete the decision-maker names and contact details before
> outreach. Public company websites and LinkedIn are the recommended
> sources.
>
> **Last updated:** 2026-10-16

---

## How to use this list

1. Complete the contact fields for the top 10 companies first.
2. Send the initial outreach email (`docs/26-OUTREACH-EMAILS.md`).
3. Sign NDA before sharing the Investment Memorandum.
4. For non-responders, follow up once after 10 days, then move on.

## Tier definitions

- **Tier 1 — Immediate fit:** hospitality operators large enough to
  absorb the asset and with an existing technology function.
- **Tier 2 — Strong fit:** regional operators with growth plans and
  an interest in owning technology.
- **Tier 3 — Strategic fit:** tech companies or investment vehicles
  that could either use or resell the platform.
